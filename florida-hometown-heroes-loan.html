Florida Hometown Heroes Loans

★★★★★5.0 from 28 verified Google reviews · Melbourne, FL · family-owned since 1999 · NMLS #353790

Hometown Heroes is Florida Housing’s program to help first-time buyers who work in Florida with down payment and closing costs. It is popular, the money is limited, and it is reserved first-come, first-served once you are under contract. The buyers who get it are usually the ones whose paperwork was ready before they signed.

How the Program Works

Hometown Heroes is run by Florida Housing Finance Corporation, the state’s housing finance agency. The help comes as a second mortgage that sits alongside a Florida Housing first mortgage and goes toward your down payment and closing costs. It is not a grant: it is repaid later, typically when you sell, refinance or pay off the first mortgage.

Florida Housing sets the amount of help, the repayment terms, the income limits and the minimum credit score, and it can change them from one funding year to the next. Rather than repeat numbers here that may be out of date, we check the current program guide against your situation and show you the terms in writing. You can read the program description on Florida Housing’s website.

Who It’s For

The program is built for Florida’s workforce. In general that means:

  • a first-time homebuyer,
  • working full-time for a Florida-based employer,
  • buying a primary residence in Florida,
  • with household income under the limit for your county, and
  • a credit score that meets the program minimum.

Self-employed buyers and people who work partly from home have their own rules in the current guide. If you are not sure where you fit, that is exactly the conversation to have before you start touring homes.

The Part People Miss: Timing

Funding is limited and set aside first-come, first-served, and a reservation can only be made once you have a signed purchase contract. When a year’s money is committed, new reservations stop until Florida Housing approves more.

So the order matters. Get prequalified and have your documents in hand first, then shop. When you sign a contract, the file is ready to reserve the same day instead of a week later.

We place Hometown Heroes loans through a lender Florida Housing has approved, and we compare it against what else you qualify for. Sometimes a different Florida Housing program, or a regular FHA, VA or conventional loan, is the better fit, and we will tell you when it is.

Common Questions

Hometown Heroes Questions

Who runs the Hometown Heroes program?

Florida Housing Finance Corporation, the state's housing finance agency. It sets the rules, the income limits and the amount of help, and it decides when funding opens and closes. Homesite Mortgage is a licensed mortgage broker, not Florida Housing. We place Hometown Heroes loans through a lender Florida Housing has approved, and we are not affiliated with or endorsed by Florida Housing.

Who qualifies?

The program is aimed at Florida's workforce: generally first-time homebuyers who work full-time for a Florida-based employer, buying a primary residence in Florida, with household income under the limit for their county and a credit score that meets the program minimum. The exact rules change from year to year, so we check your situation against the current program guide rather than a summary.

Is the assistance a grant?

No. Hometown Heroes help comes as a second mortgage alongside a Florida Housing first mortgage, and it is repaid later, typically when you sell, refinance or pay off the first mortgage. Florida Housing sets the amount and the repayment terms. We will show you the current terms in writing for your situation before you commit to anything.

What kind of first mortgage goes with it?

A Florida Housing first mortgage, which can be FHA, VA, USDA or conventional depending on your situation. The Hometown Heroes assistance cannot be stacked with Florida Housing's other down payment programs, so part of the job is working out which combination actually fits you best.

Why do people miss out on it?

Timing. The money is limited and is set aside first-come, first-served, and only once you have a signed purchase contract on a home. When a year's funding is used up, new reservations stop until more is approved. If you think you might qualify, talk to us before you go under contract so the paperwork is ready to move the day you sign.
